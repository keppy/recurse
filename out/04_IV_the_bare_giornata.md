# IV / THE BARE GIORNATA

I keep twelve giornate, each dated by row and column, each join ruled and textured, each patch of plaster accounted for from arriccio to gold leaf. I have no column for what the sinopia uncovers once the twelfth is finally painted, no line for a face where I expected only pigment, and the margin only says rank of the finished wall: one.

**alt:** A twelve-cell fresco wall grid, rows 1-3 and columns a-d, textured like plaster with a thick diagonal scar line running from the top-left corner to the bottom-right. The bottom-right cell (giornata 12) has a dotted texture with two black dots and a curved stroke suggesting a face, labeled 'it is a face' and 'the twelfth is here.' A legend above shows gold leaf, a spiral stair, and a dotted 'kernel' swatch. The word 'EMPTY' is struck through at bottom left, and a verdict reads 'GIORNATA XII: RANK ONE.'
