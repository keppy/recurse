# I / THE CARDAMOM RECKONING

I record fourteen pods at 1.4 g each, nineteen point six grams entered and a basket left untouched beside them, then seven hands of returns tallied by pod, weight, and turns until the seventh is struck under 'returned' and six came down. There is no column for why the cloth stayed folded or why the rock keeps three names and no wart; the margin says all holdings accounted for.
