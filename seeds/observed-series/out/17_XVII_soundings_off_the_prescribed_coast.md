# XVII / SOUNDINGS OFF THE PRESCRIBED COAST

The sheet records four stations along the prescribed coast, a compass, and eleven soundings dropped where nobody asked for depth; the lead came back wet every time and the register has no line for that. The one bearing it managed to fix is twelve degrees down, and in the margin someone has written that the crumb is still listed.
